SPARE TIME · STORY 1

Noah

Sport and Time to Rest
📖 Read

I like moving around after school. I spend a lot of the day sitting in a classroom. After that, I want to move! There are lots of things I enjoy. Swimming is my favourite, but I also like football and games with my friends.

At the pool, I often practise swimming faster. I also practise turning at the end of the pool. I need to put my feet against the wall and push hard. Sometimes it goes well. Sometimes I get water in my mouth instead. That part isn't much fun!

I like seeing small changes in my swimming. Perhaps I can swim a little faster than before. Perhaps I can swim further without stopping. I don't need to win something every time. It's good to know that I'm getting better. Of course, winning a race is nice too!

I sometimes swim in races. Before a race, I feel a little afraid and look at the other swimmers. Some of them look very fast! Then the race starts, and I have to think about my own swimming. I can't watch everyone else and swim well at the same time.

Training can be hard. Some days, I feel tired and don't swim very well. I still try, but I'm happy when I can stop. After that, I'm always hungry. I like swimming, but I really like eating after swimming! A small snack usually isn't enough for me.

I often play football with my mates too. We can play in the park with a ball and some jumpers for goals. We don't always have the same number of players. That's all right. We can change the teams or make the goals smaller.

We all want to score, but we like laughing too. Sometimes I miss an easy goal. My friends think that's very funny. Then one of them does the same thing, and it's my turn to laugh. We can enjoy a game even when we make mistakes.

When it's very wet outside, we sometimes play a game indoors or watch television. I like that too. After a while, I often want to go outside again. But if the rain is really heavy, I'm happy to stay inside. Wet socks are never much fun.

At home, I enjoy watching football and swimming on television. I watch how the swimmers move and turn. Then I try to do it myself at the pool. It usually looks much easier on television! I also like relaxing in comfortable clothes with something to eat.

I don't want to be busy every minute. After sport, I can enjoy a quiet evening on the sofa. On my favourite free days, I do both. I can swim or play football, see my mates and relax after that. That's a good day for me.

SPARE TIME · STORY 2

Abby

Stories, Music and More
📖 Read

I enjoy having time to do things I like. Some afternoons, I want to make something or write a story. Other afternoons, I just want music and a long chat with my friends. I don't always know what I want to do until I get home!

Writing stories is one of my favourite things. I can get an idea from a photograph or something someone says. Then I start thinking about the people in my story. Who are they? Where do they live? I can spend a long time thinking about their lives.

Starting a story is easier than finishing one. Sometimes I get another idea before I finish the first story. Then I want to write that one instead! I have to choose one and keep writing. If I don't finish them, none of my stories has an ending!

I don't show everyone my writing. I sometimes worry that people won't like it. But my friends can read it. I like hearing what they think. If they ask about the next part, I feel really pleased. It means they want to read more!

I also enjoy drawing and making things for my bedroom. I like choosing colours and trying something new. It doesn't always look how I want. Sometimes there's paper everywhere and paint on my fingers. I still have fun, but putting everything away is less exciting.

Music is usually on when I'm at home. I listen while I'm drawing or getting ready. I even listen when I'm tidying my room. That job takes longer because I often stop to sing. Then I find an old photograph and stop again. Mum isn't surprised!

My friends and I take photos and make funny videos too. We don't share all of them. Some are just for us. We don't always plan the funniest ones. Someone forgets what to do, starts laughing and makes everyone else laugh. We can watch those videos again and again.

I love being near the sea when I can. I like surfing, but I'm still learning. In the water, I have to think about my board and the waves. I can't spend all my time worrying about my hair. It's already wet, and that's fine!

Most afternoons, I stay closer to home. My friends and I might walk round the shops or sit and talk. We don't have to buy anything. I also enjoy time in my room on my own. I can choose the music and draw or write for a while.

I like having different things to do in my free time. I don't need a plan for every afternoon. Sometimes I make something I'm really happy with. Sometimes I spend two hours talking about nothing very important. Both can be fun. The tidying can wait a little longer!

SPARE TIME · STORY 3

Ameena

Time to Choose
📖 Read

I don't always have much spare time. There's school, homework and a lot to do at home. So I'm happy when I have a free afternoon! I like choosing what to do myself. Sometimes I want to play sport. Sometimes I just want some time in a quiet place.

I often meet my friends in the park. We might play a game or run around for a while. I enjoy moving after a long day in class. If we're tired, we can sit on the grass instead. We don't have to do the same thing every time.

I like trying new games. Sometimes I ask about the rules before we start. Other times, I watch my friends and learn while I play. We don't always agree about the rules! Then we choose something simple and start again. I want time to play, not just talk.

Climbing is something else I enjoy. I tried it at my summer camp and wanted to do more. At an indoor climbing wall, I choose where to put my hands and feet. There are different ways to climb up. Some are much harder than others.

I like finding a way to get higher. Sometimes I can't find a good place for my foot. I have to stop and look carefully. I don't always get to the top. I'm not happy about that, but I can try again. I feel really pleased when I get a little further.

I like quiet activities too. I enjoy reading or listening to music when I have time. In our busy house, I sometimes need to find a quiet place first. Then I can sit comfortably and enjoy my book. It's nice when nobody needs anything for a while!

I'm interested in the human body because I want to become a doctor. I sometimes read about how our bodies work. There are lots of things I don't know yet. I enjoy finding answers, but I don't study every free minute. I already have enough homework!

I also help with ideas to make our school better. I do this with a group of students. We sometimes meet after lessons. We think about ideas and things students want to change. I like doing that, but I also need a break from school. When I'm with my friends, we can talk about music or something funny instead.

I can't always stay in the park as long as everyone else. Sometimes I need to help at home or finish some work. I like knowing the time I need to leave before I go out. Then I can enjoy being there without looking at the clock all afternoon.

A good afternoon doesn't have to cost much. I can have fun near home with a few friends. I like moving around, trying something interesting and having time to choose. A quiet half hour with a book is good too. There isn't a rule that I must be busy!

SPARE TIME · STORY 4

Luca

Sport, Friends and Relaxing
📖 Read

Do you know what I love about spare time? You can do whatever you feel like doing! Sometimes I want to meet my friends or play sports, but sometimes I just want to relax and do absolutely nothing. That's what spare time is for, isn't it?

After school, I often spend time with my mates Jack and Harry. Sometimes we go to the park, play games or just hang out together. We don't always do anything special. We can spend ages talking and laughing about the smallest things. Harry usually makes us laugh, and Jack always seems to know something the rest of us don't.

I also play volleyball. I'm in a team and I really enjoy our training sessions and matches. I love serving, especially when the ball goes exactly where I want it to go. Of course, that doesn't always happen! But I don't mind losing a match sometimes. As long as we've played well together, I'm usually happy.

My little brother Oliver plays hockey. Our sports clubs are quite close to each other, so when our matches aren't at the same time, I sometimes go and watch him play. I really enjoy cheering him on, especially when his team scores. Oliver sometimes comes to my volleyball matches too. It's nice having someone in the family supporting you!

At weekends, my family and I often go to our local swimming club. My parents, Oliver and I are all members. It's not a competition club. We don't train for races or try to swim faster than everyone else. People just come to swim, relax and enjoy spending time together.

I've also made some good friends there. There are a few boys around my age, and we often swim together or play games in the water. Sometimes we just sit by the pool and have a chat.

One thing about our club is that some teenagers stop coming when they get older. I suppose growing up can be a bit strange. Your body starts changing, and some people become quite shy about that. I can understand it, even though I've always felt comfortable at the club.

That's one reason why the club organises special activities for young members once a month. They want teenagers to keep enjoying the club and spending time together.

Last Saturday, they put a small inflatable obstacle course in the swimming pool. Our pool isn't very big, so the course wasn't huge either. But it was great fun! Oliver and I spent the afternoon climbing over slippery obstacles, falling into the water and laughing with our friends. It was a brilliant afternoon!

Of course, I don't spend all my spare time with other people. I also love relaxing at home, whether it's a quiet afternoon after school or an evening with nothing planned. I put on one of my comfortable onesies and relax on the sofa. Sometimes I watch a film, listen to music or just enjoy doing absolutely nothing. I can easily spend a whole afternoon like that!

I think that's the best thing about my spare time. I can be with my friends, enjoy sports, spend time with my family or simply relax at home. And I wouldn't want it any other way!

SPARE TIME · STORY 5

Raheem

A Closer Look
📖 Read

I enjoy walking, taking photographs and finding things out. That may not sound very exciting, but I like it. I don't always need a big plan for my free time. I can go outside, choose a direction and see what I find. There's usually something interesting to look at.

I often see small things near home. There might be a date above a door or an old sign on a wall. Then I want to know more. How old is the building? Who lived there before? I can think of a lot of questions during one short walk!

Taking photographs helps me look carefully. I sometimes take a picture from two or three different places. A building can look quite different from the other side of the street. My friends don't always understand why it takes so long. I think they're happier when I photograph them!

I like pictures of unusual things too. They don't all have to be beautiful. An empty chair in a strange place can be interesting. Why is it there? Did someone forget it? I don't know the answer, but I still like having the photograph.

At home, I look through my pictures. Some are better than others. Sometimes I see something new behind the people in a photo. Then I want to go back and look again. I don't keep every photograph. My phone doesn't need twenty pictures of the same door!

I also enjoy looking for information about places. I might read about an old building or find a street on a map. I don't do this after every walk. Sometimes I just go home and have something to eat. Questions are interesting, but lunch is important too.

I like films with a good story. I enjoy thinking about what might happen next. Sometimes I'm right. Sometimes the ending is completely different from my idea. That's all right. I don't want every film to be easy to guess. Where's the fun in that?

I play games too. I like games where I have to think and find answers. My friends sometimes choose faster games. I play too, but I need time to learn what to do. They're often playing before I understand all the buttons. Then I have to learn quickly!

I also like time without my phone or a television. I can read, listen to music or go for a walk. I don't take pictures every time I'm outside. Sometimes it's nice to look at something and leave my phone in my pocket. I can remember it myself.

At school, the teacher usually chooses what we learn about. In my spare time, I can choose what I read about. I like choosing for myself. I can look at a map for twenty minutes if I want. My sister might find that boring, but she can choose something else!

SPARE TIME · STORY 6

Mia

Time for Things I Enjoy
📖 Read

I like having free time after school. Sometimes I have lots of ideas for the afternoon. I want to go outside, make something and listen to music. I don't always do everything. If I really enjoy one activity, I can spend a long time on that instead.

Being outside is good after a day in class. I like walking, playing games and spending time near water. I don't need to go far every time. A little fresh air can help when I'm tired of sitting. I often feel better after moving around for a while.

I enjoy activities where someone can show me what to do. Long instructions are harder for me. I might remember the first part and forget the next part. If I can watch and try it myself, it's usually easier. Then I want to start!

I would like to go canoeing again. I enjoy being on the water and doing something with my hands. But I can't do that every afternoon. Most days, I stay closer to home. A walk or a simple game can still be fun. I don't need a big adventure.

With my friends, I like music, talking and doing things outside. Sometimes we do a silly dance to a song. We don't have to be good at it. We can laugh and try again. I don't always want a game where someone wins. Enjoying it is enough.

At home, I sometimes draw or make something. I like seeing it change as I work. I might spend a long time choosing a colour or making one small part. Then I look at the time. I can be very surprised! I often think I started five minutes ago!

It helps to choose one thing first. If I get out too many things, my table becomes a mess. Then I can't find what I need. I try to leave some space and finish one part before starting another. It doesn't always work, but I keep trying.

I enjoy listening to music on my own too. Sometimes I play a song again because I love one part. Other times, I want the room to be quiet. I feel different on different days. I don't always need the same thing to help me relax.

I've got ADHD, but that doesn't mean I want to be busy every second. After school, I can feel very tired. At school, I try hard to listen and remember things. Sometimes I just want comfortable clothes, the sofa and something to watch. I need rest too!

I like free time when I can enjoy myself and don't have to hurry. Sometimes Mum or Dad has to tell me it's time to stop. But I don't want to worry about that all afternoon. I want time to have fun, just like my friends.
